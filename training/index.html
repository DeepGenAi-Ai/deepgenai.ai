Flagship Programme

Six Sigma Belt Training.

Structured, role-based Lean Six Sigma training that builds process improvement capability at every level of your organisation — from front-line team members to full-time improvement leaders.

Yellow Belt

An introduction to Lean Six Sigma and the DMAIC problem-solving cycle for team members and project support staff who need to understand and contribute to improvement projects.

Green Belt

For team leads and process owners who run improvement projects alongside their day-to-day role — covering root-cause analysis, statistical tools and project management within DMAIC.

Black Belt

Advanced statistical and change-leadership skills for practitioners who lead complex, cross-functional improvement projects and mentor Green Belts across the business.

Delivered in-company or as open enrolment — see Contact for upcoming dates.
